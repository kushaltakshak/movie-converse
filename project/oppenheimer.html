Movie Synopsis

The story of American scientist J. Robert Oppenheimer and his role in the development of the atomic bomb during World War II. Appointed as the director of the Los Alamos Laboratory during the Manhattan Project, Oppenheimer leads an ensemble of the world's most brilliant physicists to build a weapon that changes the destiny of humanity forever. The film swept the 96th Academy Awards, taking home seven Oscars including Best Picture, Best Director, and Best Actor.
