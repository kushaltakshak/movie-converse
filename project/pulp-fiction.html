Movie Synopsis

The lives of two mob hitmen, a boxer, a gangster and his wife, and a pair of diner bandits intertwine in four tales of violence and redemption. Written and directed by Quentin Tarantino, this non-linear crime masterpiece won the Palme d'Or at Cannes and the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay.
