Movie Synopsis

Andy Dufresne, a successful young banker, is convicted of murdering his wife and her lover and sentenced to two consecutive life terms at Shawshank State Penitentiary. Over twenty years, he develops an enduring bond with fellow inmate Red, becomes instrumental in prison operations, and secretly holds onto the invincible power of hope and human resilience.
